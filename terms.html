1. What this app is

Earth Timeline is an educational visualisation. It shows approximate reconstructions of Earth's continents at selected points in geological history, and estimates where a present-day place sat at those times.

2. Accuracy — please read this one

The reconstructions are scientific models, not measurements, and are presented for interest and education.

  • Plate positions come from published plate-motion models and carry real uncertainty that grows the further back you go. Deep-time positions (hundreds of millions of years) are actively debated among researchers.
  • Coastlines and climate figures shown alongside each epoch are simplified summaries, not precise values for a specific date.
  • A reconstructed position is the position of the tectonic plate, not evidence that any particular land, feature, or environment existed there.

Do not rely on this app for academic, professional, legal, navigational, commercial, or safety-critical purposes. For research use, go to the underlying models and cite them directly.

3. Third-party services

The app sends the place you search for to Google's geocoding service, and the resulting coordinates to the GPlates Web Service operated by EarthByte (University of Sydney). Your use of the app therefore also involves those services. We do not control them, cannot guarantee their availability, and are not responsible for their content or behaviour.

If those services are unavailable, the app falls back to a coarse built-in approximation, which is less accurate and may disagree noticeably with the maps shown.
